Good decisions are rarely accidental. These are the principles I bring to learning, trading, and building AJT77.

01 /

Read the context

Start with the bigger picture. Understand market structure, liquidity, and the conditions around a setup before looking for an entry.

02 /

Protect the downside

Risk management comes before reward. A plan matters only when its invalidation, exposure, and limits are understood.

03 /

Review honestly

Every trade can teach something. Keep records, study mistakes without excuses, and let evidence improve the next decision.

My approach

Less noise.
More process.

I’m interested in building a repeatable process, not chasing every move. My framework centres on preparation, confirmation, risk, and review. Markets remain uncertain, so every idea needs clear conditions and controlled risk.

01

Build the higher-timeframe view

Study weekly and daily context, directional pressure, key zones, and relevant liquidity.

02

Refine the area of interest

Compare the 4H and 1H structure with the larger idea. If the picture conflicts, wait.

03

Wait for confirmation

Look for a defined entry trigger instead of entering from expectation alone.

04

Manage risk and review

Know the invalidation point, respect daily limits, and record the outcome without hindsight bias.
